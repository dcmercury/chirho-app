## Design Context

### Users
Christians using a mobile prayer app for personal prayer, loved-one intercession, and small prayer-group participation. The interface should remain clear and reassuring during reflective use.

### Brand Personality
Reverent, calm, and refined. The experience should feel distinctly Christian without becoming ornate, loud, or visually theatrical.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, immersive photographic surfaces with warm Chi-Rho imagery, restrained orange accents, strong text legibility, compact native controls, and consistent Space Grotesk, Inter, and JetBrains Mono typography.

### Design Principles
- Preserve a classy, contemplative tone.
- Prefer clear native interactions and purposeful motion.
- Keep controls compact while maintaining accessible touch targets.
- Use contrast and opacity deliberately so content remains legible.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid decorative animation fatigue.
